Cyber Crime Portal

Learn About Cybercrime

In general cybercrime may be defined as “Any unlawful act where computer or communication device or computer network is used to commit or facilitate the commission of crime”.

Below is a list of some of the cybercrimes along with their indicative explanation. This is to facilitate better reporting of complaints.

1. Child Pornography / Child Sexually Abusive Material (CSAM) ▼
Publishing, transmitting, browsing, or storing any content involving sexually explicit acts with a child is a serious criminal offense under the Information Technology Act and IPC.
2. Cyber Bullying ▼
Cyber bullying involves harassment or threatening behavior conducted through digital platforms such as social media, emails, messaging apps, or online forums.
3. Cyber Stalking ▼
Cyber stalking refers to repeated online harassment or monitoring of an individual using digital communication tools, causing fear or distress.
4. Cyber Grooming ▼
Establishing an emotional connection with a minor online to gain trust for exploitation or abuse.
5. Online Job Fraud ▼
Fake job offers or employment scams used to collect money or personal information from victims.
6. Online Sextortion ▼
Threatening to publish private images or videos unless money or other favors are provided.
7. Vishing ▼
Fraudulent phone calls pretending to be bank officials or authorities to obtain sensitive financial information.
8. Sexting ▼
Sexting is an act of sending sexually explicit digital images, videos, text messages, or emails, usually by cell phone.
9. Smshing ▼
Smishing is a type of fraud that uses mobile phone text messages to lure victims into calling back on a fraudulent phone number, visiting fraudulent websites or downloading malicious content via phone or web.
10. SIM Swap Scam ▼
SIM Swap Scam occurs when fraudsters manage to get a new SIM card issued against a registered mobile number fraudulently through the mobile service provider. With the help of this new SIM card, they get One Time Password (OTP) and alerts, required for making financial transactions through victim's bank account. Getting a new SIM card against a registered mobile number fraudulently is known as SIM Swap.
