Cyber Crime Portal

Learn About Cybercrime

In general cybercrime may be defined as “Any unlawful act where computer or communication device or computer network is used to commit or facilitate the commission of crime”.

Below is a list of some of the cybercrimes along with their indicative explanation. This is to facilitate better reporting of complaints.

1. Child Pornography / CSAM ▼
Publishing or storing sexually explicit content involving minors is a serious offense under IT Act and IPC.
2. Cyber Bullying ▼
Online harassment through social media, emails, or messaging platforms.
3. Cyber Stalking ▼
Repeated monitoring or harassment using digital tools causing fear or distress.
4. Cyber Grooming ▼
Building emotional connection with minors online for exploitation.
5. Online Job Fraud ▼
Fake job offers to steal money or personal data.
6. Online Sextortion ▼
Threatening to leak private images for money or favors.
7. Vishing ▼
Fraud phone calls pretending to be bank officials to steal financial data.
8. Sexting ▼
Sending sexually explicit content via mobile or internet.
9. Smishing ▼
Fraud SMS with malicious links or fake numbers.
10. SIM Swap Scam ▼
Fraudsters get duplicate SIM to receive OTP and access bank accounts.
